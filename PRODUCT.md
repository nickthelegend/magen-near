# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Vite + React, TypeScript signing SDK, Rust near-sdk contract; specified by Nick.
## Users
NEAR account holders; hackathon judges evaluating a real wallet without crypto expertise.
## Product Purpose
Your NEAR account, safe from quantum computers. Create, receive, send, inspect activity, and migrate account control to a native post-quantum key.
## Positioning
A locked contract wallet requiring ed25519 AND ML-DSA-65 signatures on each action, plus an explicit native-key migration.
## Constraints
Testnet only. Real chain receipts only. No claims of audit or whole-network quantum safety. Keys stay in the browser; demo keys never committed. Q-Day full-access key changes the control model to PQ-only. No guardians in v1 (time/scope choice).
## Brand Commitments
Magen means shield. Premium fintech; dark balance surface, light task surfaces, one accent. Familiar wallet navigation. No borrowed artwork. Nick requested autonomous decisions, so no interview was performed.
## Evidence on Hand
Fresh scaffold dated Nov 13. RPC status rechecked Nov 13: both networks protocol 88. All deployment claims must be backed by deployments records.
## Principles
Make the primary transfer obvious. Explain signature protection in ordinary language. Link every submitted transaction. Keep failure visible. Never present read-only access as signing authority.
